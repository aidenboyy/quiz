01 / Home energy storage

Use more of
your own solar.

When your solar panels produce more electricity than your home is using, a compatible home battery can store some of that surplus.

Use stored energy later, when your panels are producing less. The amount you can store depends on your system and household use.

02 / What a battery can do

Put stored solar energy to use.

A home battery can help you use more of the electricity your panels produce. What makes sense depends on your home.

01

Use solar after sunset

Store some of your daytime surplus and use it in the evening, subject to the battery’s available charge.

02

Shift when you use energy

A battery can help move some electricity use beyond daylight hours. Results depend on your routine and energy plan.

03

Plan around your home

Battery size and compatibility depend on your solar installation, electricity use and available space.

03 / How it works

Three steps to stored solar power.

01 — DAYTIME

Solar powers your home first

Your panels supply the electricity your home needs. Surplus can charge a connected battery.

02 — STORAGE

The battery stores surplus

The battery stores available excess energy, up to its capacity and charging limits.

03 — LATER

Use stored energy later

When solar production drops, your home can use the stored electricity. The grid can supply any shortfall.
